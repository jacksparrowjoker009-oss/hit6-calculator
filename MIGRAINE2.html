ICHD-3 Diagnostic Criteria – Episodic Migraine

Review criteria below and select confirmed diagnostic classification.

1.1 Migraine without aura
  • A. At least 5 attacks fulfilling criteria B–D
  • B. Attacks lasting 4–72 hours (untreated or unsuccessfully treated)
  • C. Headache has ≥2 of the following 4:
    1. Unilateral location
    2. Pulsating quality
    3. Moderate or severe pain intensity
    4. Aggravation by routine physical activity
  • D. During headache ≥1 of the following:
    1. Nausea and/or vomiting
    2. Photophobia and phonophobia
  • E. Not better accounted for by another diagnosis
1.2 Migraine with aura
  • A. At least 2 attacks fulfilling criteria B and C
  • B. ≥1 reversible aura symptom:
    1. Visual / Sensory / Speech-Language
    2. Motor / Brainstem / Retinal
  • C. ≥3 of the following 6 characteristics:
    1. Spreads gradually over ≥5 minutes
    2. 2+ aura symptoms occur in succession
    3. Each aura symptom lasts 5–60 minutes
    4. Unilateral / Positive symptom
    5. Accompanied/followed within 60 min by headache
  • D. Not better accounted for by another diagnosis
