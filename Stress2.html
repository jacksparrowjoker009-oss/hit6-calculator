Clinical Assessment Battery

Consolidated evaluation covering Acute Stress (VAS), 30-Day Perceived Stress (PSS-10), and Sleep Quality (PSQI).

Assess your current acute stress level along the 0–100 mm continuous scale.

2. Perceived Stress Scale (PSS-10) — 30-Day Appraisal
Rate your feelings over the last month (0 = Never to 4 = Very Often).
